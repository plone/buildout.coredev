request.other (via item access) rather than on request.__dict__.

Commit: https://github.com/plone/plone.registry/commit/a25c27d540bdd1e88f2f1bd3f359a0d8806e1dac

Use request.other directly instead of request item access

request["key"] pollutes dict-based test stubs (like plone.caching's
DummyRequest(dict)) breaking their dict(request) assertions.
Use request.other directly - the plain dict that Zope's HTTPRequest
clears at end-of-request. Gracefully skip caching when other is
not available (zope.publisher TestRequest, dict-based stubs).
