Fix IndexError scaling images with non-RGB color modes

The greyscale optimisation assumed getcolors() returns 3-element RGB
tuples, but modes like PA (palette+alpha) and LA (greyscale+alpha)
return 2-element tuples, causing IndexError on rgb[2].

Restrict the greyscale check to RGB/RGBA modes where the tuple
structure is guaranteed.

Fixes #34
