Preserve source order of Diazo head resources

Copy metadata, links, styles, and scripts with a single selection so import maps remain ahead of modulepreload links and module scripts. Separate tag groups caused Firefox to reject import maps and fail bare-specifier resolution.

Cover the compiled Diazo output, including resource order, attributes, title/base/icons, and notheme conditions. Verify preload-enabled fixtures in Firefox and Chromium.
