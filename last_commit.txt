Add munge_search_term to plone.base.utils

Move search term munging utilities (munge_search_term, quote, quote_chars,
BAD_CHARS, MULTISPACE) from Products.CMFPlone and plone.app.querystring
to plone.base.utils as the canonical location.

Fix multi-word search: apply wildcard to all unquoted terms, not just
the last one. "super expi" now produces "super* AND expi*" instead of
"super AND expi*".

Refs plone/Products.CMFPlone#4205
