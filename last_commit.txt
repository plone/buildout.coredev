Merge "is not" criteria on the same index instead of overwriting them

A single "getId is not xxx" criterion worked, but two rows "getId is not
xxx" and "getId is not yyy" excluded only yyy: parseFormquery lets the
last row on an index overwrite the previous one (ranges being the only
exception), so every further "is not" row was silently dropped. The same
happened when an exclusion was combined with a positive selection on the
same index, e.g. "review_state is published or pending" plus "review_state
is not pending".

The parser now folds exclusions into one record per index: several
"is not" rows become one "not" list, and an exclusion next to a selection
or range yields "query" plus "not", which the catalog supports. Repeated
positive selections keep the last-wins behaviour.
