Restrict module security declarations to explicit public names

Backport of the fix for GHSA-92qw-564p-pmg6 from master to 6.1.x.

Replace allow_module() on Products.CMFPlone.utils and
Products.CMFPlone.browser.navtree with per-name declarePublic(), and
drop allow_module() on OFS.ObjectManager. Opening a whole module also
published every name it imports. Make sure AccessControl.allow_class
and allow_type can't be called from restricted code. Protect more
modules. Add regression tests.

Co-authored-by: Mauro Amico &lt;mauro.amico@gmail.com&gt;
